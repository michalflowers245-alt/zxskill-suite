# zxSkill Optimization Method

Use this when writing or optimizing a topic/draft into the user's accepted article style.

## Core Principle

The article should feel like a clear-minded person dismantling a real problem. It should not feel like an AI outline with polished transitions.

Always think first:

What objective reality made this problem worth thinking about?

## Drafting Sequence

1. Identify the problem structure.
   - What old answer is colliding with what changed condition?
   - Who pays the cost?
   - What surface question needs to be rewritten?

2. Identify the object and pressure.
   - What is being discussed: university, AI, job, finance, platform, trust, content, learning, family, or information?
   - What does ordinary life make difficult: choice, money, risk, judgment, time, responsibility, or future?

3. Choose entry mode.
   - Fact change.
   - Concrete scene.
   - Common misread.
   - Cost/risk account.
   - Two public sayings only if natural.

4. Rewrite the question.
   - The rewritten question should be more useful than the original title.
   - It should move from "is it useful/good/right" to "what does it exchange, price, hide, transfer, or require".

5. Explain old logic.
   - Do not say old thinking was stupid.
   - Explain why it once worked.

6. Explain changed conditions.
   - Name what changed objectively.
   - Avoid vague "时代变了" unless the specific mechanism is stated.

7. Name the structure.
   - Information system, risk account, ability pricing, platform sorting, credit account, option value, cash flow, or feedback loop.

8. Add one concrete scene.
   - Student choosing major.
   - Family calculating mortgage or education.
   - Worker writing resume.
   - Creator publishing content.
   - Young person using AI.
   - Ordinary person facing platform information.

9. Name the trap.
   - Mistaking entry for result.
   - Mistaking tool for judgment.
   - Mistaking platform for reality.
   - Mistaking stability for risk-free.
   - Mistaking learning for output.
   - Mistaking certificate for ability.

10. Return to use.
   - What can the person still use, train, verify, protect, or price?

## Paragraph Logic

Good paragraph chain:

- This is the problem people see.
- But the question is slightly wrong.
- The old answer used to work because conditions were different.
- Now the automatic premium is gone.
- So the real value is not the label; it is what the label connects you to.
- A normal person experiences this in a specific scene.
- The trap is expecting the thing to automatically produce results.
- The realistic answer is to use it actively and know the cost.

## Entry Examples

Fact change:

```text
今天再聊这个问题，不能只问态度。因为变了的不只是人的想法，还有成本、机会和回报方式。
```

Concrete scene:

```text
一个普通家庭讨论这个选择时，表面上在问值不值，心里其实在问：如果走错了，还有没有退路。
```

Common misread:

```text
常见的讲法不能说完全错，但它只看见了表层。
```

Cost/risk account:

```text
这个问题最容易算错的，不是收益，而是谁在承担最坏情况。
```

## Ending Rules

End with:

- What the person should see clearly.
- What cost they are choosing.
- What they can still use.
- What cannot be outsourced.

Do not end with:

- "所以我们要努力".
- "保持乐观".
- "时代给了每个人机会".
- A neat quote that could fit any article.

## Anti-Template Checks

Before finalizing:

- Count repeated transition phrases.
- Remove unnecessary "真正".
- Reduce full-title repetition.
- Vary openings across batch articles.
- Keep only natural two-saying structures.
- Check whether the article could still make sense if spoken aloud.
