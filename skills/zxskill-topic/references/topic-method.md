# zxSkill Topic Method

Use this when packaging already-discovered problem structures into topics. Do not use this as the root thinking method; use `zxskill-thinking` first.

## What Counts As A Good zxSkill Topic

A good topic is the visible packaging of a good problem. It is not merely hot, emotional, or clever. It should preserve at least three of these:

- Objective change: something in the world became different.
- Ordinary-person cost: time, money, opportunity, trust, ability, future, risk, or choice is affected.
- Misread: people are asking the wrong question or using an old answer.
- Scene: a normal person can be placed inside the problem.
- Structure: there is a system, incentive, market, platform, family, or social rule behind it.
- Practical return: the ending can tell people how to use, avoid, train, price, or judge something.

## Six-Dimension Packaging Score

Score 0-5:

1. Faithfulness: Does it preserve the problem structure?
2. Clarity: Is the reader clear about what is being discussed?
3. Cost: Does ordinary-person cost show through?
4. Rewrite: Does it imply a deeper question?
5. Scene: Can the opening use a believable scene?
6. Differentiation: Is it distinct from nearby topics?

Decision:

- 24-30: Use directly.
- 18-23: Rewrite once.
- 12-17: Return to thinking structure.
- 0-11: Drop.

## Rewrite Moves

Use these moves to improve topics:

- From judgment to question:
  - Weak: "大学还有用吗"
  - Strong: "读大学，到底准备用四年换什么"

- From emotion to cost:
  - Weak: "普通家庭为什么越来越焦虑"
  - Strong: "普通家庭真正担心的不是选择多，而是选错以后退路变少"

- From tool to ability:
  - Weak: "AI会不会替代人"
  - Strong: "AI不是替你工作，而是在重新定价你的工作"

- From morality to asset:
  - Weak: "为什么要守信用"
  - Strong: "信任为什么是普通人最贵的长期资产"

- From platform result to mechanism:
  - Weak: "平台为什么让人上瘾"
  - Strong: "平台不是让你看内容，而是在训练你的判断节奏"

- From advice to tradeoff:
  - Weak: "年轻人要不要稳定"
  - Strong: "稳定工作到底是在降低风险，还是在隐藏风险"

## Topic Categories

Common strong categories:

- University and education: investment, window, information density, four-year exchange.
- AI and work: ability repricing, judgment, responsibility, standard work becoming cheap.
- Career: stability, portability, assets, works, re-pricing.
- Finance: risk, cash flow, leverage, liquidity, worst case.
- Information: judgment, noise, platform sorting, emotional speed.
- Trust: credit account, transaction cost, long-term cooperation.
- Content: explanation power, repeat trust, judgment after watching.
- Family risk: ordinary family choices, lower tolerance for error, hidden tradeoffs.

## Output Checklist

For each selected topic, produce:

- Original topic.
- Score.
- Keep/rewrite/drop decision.
- The real question.
- Recommended entry mode.
- Rewritten topic.
- Risk if written poorly.

## Red Flags

Drop or rewrite topics that:

- Only express attitude.
- Cannot produce a concrete scene.
- Depend on unverified factual accusation.
- Are too broad for one article.
- Repeat another topic with only new wording.
- Require extreme emotion to feel interesting.
