# zxSkill Thinking Method

This is the root of zxSkill. Topic selection, drafting, and AI cleanup all come after this.

## Root Principle

Start from a problem structure, not a topic.

A topic is packaging. A problem is the thing being thought through.

Old order to avoid:

1. Pick a content category.
2. Generate titles.
3. Force an article structure.

Correct order:

1. Notice a real mismatch in ordinary life.
2. Identify what changed.
3. Find what old answer people are still using.
4. Locate who pays the cost.
5. Rewrite the question.
6. Only then package it into a topic.

## What Makes A Problem Worth Thinking About

A strong problem has five layers:

1. Surface anxiety.
   - People are talking about it, worrying about it, copying answers, or avoiding a decision.

2. Old answer.
   - There is a familiar belief:读书有用、稳定最好、努力会被看见、信息越多越自由、会表达就能做内容、会用AI就有优势.

3. Changed condition.
   - The environment that supported the old belief has changed.

4. Hidden cost.
   - Ordinary people now pay with time, money, option value, trust, attention, risk, or responsibility.

5. Useful reframing.
   - The problem can be converted into a better question that helps people judge.

## Problem Archetypes

Use these as thinking lenses, not as titles.

### Automatic Premium Disappears

Something once carried automatic value, but no longer does.

Examples:

- 文凭仍然有用，但不再自动升值。
- 表达仍然重要，但不再因为会说就稀缺。
- 信息仍然有用，但更多信息不等于更多判断。

Question move:

- Do not ask whether it is useful.
- Ask what condition makes it useful.

### Entry Mistaken For Result

People get access to something and assume the result will follow.

Examples:

- 考上大学不等于用好大学。
- 会用AI不等于有判断。
- 有平台账号不等于有信用资产。

Question move:

- Do not ask how to get in.
- Ask what you do after entering.

### Risk Hidden By Stability

A choice feels safe because the risk is not visible yet.

Examples:

- 稳定工作隐藏了迁移能力风险。
- 房贷隐藏了现金流风险。
- 只储蓄隐藏了购买力和机会风险.

Question move:

- Do not ask whether it is stable.
- Ask what risk is being delayed or transferred.

### Tool Mistaken For Ability

People confuse having a tool with having the ability the tool amplifies.

Examples:

- AI写作不等于会思考。
- 收藏课程不等于学会。
- 数据很多不等于会判断。

Question move:

- Do not ask whether the tool is powerful.
- Ask what human ability becomes more expensive.

### Platform Sorts Reality

People mistake platform-filtered information for the whole world.

Examples:

- 算法奖励极端，不代表现实只有极端。
- 热点给了机会，也训练反应速度。
- 流量让创作者看见观众，也会训练创作者迎合。

Question move:

- Do not ask what the platform shows.
- Ask how it trains your judgment rhythm.

### Credit Account

Moral language hides an asset structure.

Examples:

- 信任不是口号，是降低交易成本的资产。
- 靠谱不是性格，是反复兑现后的信用。
- 占便宜不是小聪明，是透支未来合作额度。

Question move:

- Do not ask whether someone should be moral.
- Ask what cooperation cost changes.

### System Entrance

Something looks like a product, tool, market, or policy, but is actually an entrance into a larger system.

Examples:

- 稳定币表面是链上美元，实质是支付入口和金融标准。
- 平台表面给创作者流量，实质在分配注意力和训练创作者。
- 大学表面给文凭，实质给信息密度和试错窗口。

Question move:

- Do not ask whether the thing is hot.
- Ask what system entrance it controls.

### Cost Repackaged As Choice

People think they are choosing freely, but the available choices have already been shaped by cost, structure, or lack of alternatives.

Examples:

- 低欲望可能不是不想要，而是高成本下的自我保护。
- 普通家庭保守，不一定是胆小，而是试错空间太贵。
- 年轻人追求稳定，不一定是不敢闯，而是失败成本更难承受。

Question move:

- Do not ask why people不敢.
- Ask what cost structure made them cautious.

### Mechanism Hidden Under Emotion

Public discussion stays at anger, pride, shame, or moral judgment, but the useful question is about mechanism.

Examples:

- 预制菜争议不是单纯爱不爱吃，而是信任、标准和告知权。
- 爱国营销不是情绪本身，而是品牌把身份认同变成消费入口。
- 网暴不是单个坏人，而是平台、情绪奖励和低成本惩罚共同作用。

Question move:

- Do not stay at emotion.
- Ask what mechanism rewards that emotion.

## Media Library Reading Method

Use the media-library folder supplied by the user to identify recurring patterns in the problems described.

Suggested source interpretation:

- 老周横眉 materials: public issues, institutional mechanisms, trust collapse, nationalism, public emotion, economic pressure, moral choice.
- 小lin说 materials: economic mechanisms, finance, currency, business models, macro systems, risk transmission.
- Dan Koe materials: creator economy, personal ability stack, AI, attention, deep work, one-person business, strategic thinking.
- 学习文章 and Web3 materials: personal experiments, risk, leverage, opportunity, narrative, tools, action feedback.
- 市场调研 materials: audience demand, real-world pain, commercial context.

Extraction workflow:

1. List candidate materials.
2. For each material, ignore the title first and ask what phenomenon it records.
3. Identify the old answer embedded in public discussion.
4. Identify the changed condition.
5. Identify who pays the ordinary-person cost.
6. Assign one or two problem archetypes.
7. Rewrite the question.
8. Then package as a topic.

Output table:

```markdown
| 材料 | 现象 | 问题原型 | 旧答案 | 现实变化 | 普通人代价 | 改写后的问题 | 可包装选题 |
```

## How To Rewrite The Question

Use these moves:

- 有没有用 -> 在什么条件下有用，以及你准备用它换什么？
- 好不好 -> 它解决了什么，又把什么风险藏起来？
- 怎么做 -> 这件事真正要求你具备什么能力？
- 为什么焦虑 -> 哪个现实条件让人更难判断？
- 会不会被淘汰 -> 哪种能力正在被重新定价？
- 要不要选择 -> 最坏情况是什么，退路在哪里？

## What Not To Do

- Do not start from "100个选题".
- Do not decide the category before discovering the mismatch.
- Do not manufacture two extremes.
- Do not turn every issue into a neat opposition.
- Do not use "老周式" as a writing tone only; it is a thinking order.
- Do not pick topics that only sound correct but have no concrete cost.

## Thinking Output Example

```markdown
问题原型：入口被误当成结果
现实变化：大学文凭仍在，但自动溢价下降
旧答案：考上大学就好了
错位点：入口仍有价值，但不会自动变成回报
普通人付出的代价：四年时间、家庭投入、毕业后的选择窗口
应该改写成的问题：你准备用大学四年换什么
可包装成的题目：读大学不是上岸，而是进入一个低成本试错窗口
开头入口：一个大一新生从小地方进入更大的学校后，第一次看见实习、项目、行业和不同同龄人
是否值得写：值得
风险提醒：不要写成学历无用论，也不要写成大学万能论
```
